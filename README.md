# Studying English at CIS

A photo gallery website documenting our English language experience at CIS (Canadian International School) in Toronto, Canada.

## About

This project is a personal photo gallery that I created during a exercise of the teacher Nicole during the time that I was studying English at CIS. It showcases memorable moments and places visited during the experience, including the YMCA, Gallery of Indigenous Art, Tim Hortons, Trinity Bellwoods Park, and more.

## How to Run

Just open `index.html` in your browser — no build step or server required.
